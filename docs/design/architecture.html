Analekh Architecture

The analyst’s record, kept for you.

Every NSE-listed company is preloaded and its results are fetched from NSE’s integrated filings and parsed, on both bases, for every filer. For a company you follow, the system finds and updates your Excel model after you approve each change, or builds a new model from your sector template and drafts an initiation. Core numbers come from XBRL; the AI writes the analysis and pulls every figure from the database. The price source is still undecided.

The NSE equity and SME lists preload every company. Results come from NSE’s integrated-filing lookup: per company for the backfill and refreshes, and once a day without a ticker for the latest filings (the results feed has carried nothing since 24 August 2026). When a quarter is refiled, the most recently filed values are used and the older ones kept as history. Sectors are imported by precedence (user override, then Excel’s Stocks data type, then NSE Indices lists). Only the relevant PDF pages reach Claude. Nothing is written to Excel until the analyst approves.

What happens when an analyst enters a ticker

The ticker search runs in the browser over the whole universe, so the company opens at once. Steps 3 and 4 run only when the filing isn’t already in the database. The linked model is found through watched folders or the open workbook, identified by a hidden tag, its file name or its contents. Steps 6 and 7 are the approval gate: the analyst sees which cells change and which forecasts need rolling forward, accepts or edits each one, and only then is a new model version saved.

What changed in v0.0.3

  • Results source: NSE’s integrated-filing lookup (owner-approved), per company and once a day; the results feed stopped on 24 August 2026. History from the March 2025 quarter.
  • NSE’s limit: about 590 requests, then a pause of about 10 minutes; runs resume at the company they stopped on.
  • Formats: banks, general and life insurers detected from their line items, each with its own checks.
  • Revisions: the most recently filed values win; refiled quarters tagged Restated or Revised with what changed.
  • Web app: Results desk on Home; Latest quarter column, basis switch and lakh amounts on the company page.

What happens when an analyst starts coverage

Used when no model exists yet. The analyst picks a company and a sector template (their own upload or a built-in one). The builder fills the template’s inputs with sourced history and keeps its formulas. Proposed assumptions pass through the same approval gate before the tagged model is saved. From the next results onward, the quarterly update flow takes over.

Numbers come from data

Figures in drafts are fetched from the database or the model through tool calls, and each one carries its XBRL tag or PDF page reference.

Data rights

NSE’s published RSS feeds and lists, its integrated-filing lookup (approved by the owner; per company and once a day), NSE Indices’ constituent CSVs and company IR pages. Requests identify as Analekh. Stocks data type fields are for your own use. Written confirmation or a licence before any public release.

Your templates

Upload a model template per sector. The system reads its structure, you confirm the mapping once, and a test build must pass before it is used.

Prices (undecided)

One static reference price at a time: the day’s close after the market closes, the day’s open during market hours. Stocks data type in Excel now; NSE’s bhavcopy, which is end of day only, later.

Runs from India

NSE’s main site is geo-restricted, so the scheduler and fetchers run on an Indian network or VM. NSE stops answering after about 590 requests; runs pause 10 minutes and resume where they stopped, never working around a block.
