# IPO Coverage from RHP and Prospectus: design

Date: 2026-10-29. Status: draft for owner approval. Sources: `docs/roadmap.md` 4.1 and 5,
`docs/design/deviations.md` section 12, `CLAUDE.md` rules 1, 2, 3, 5, 8.

## Intent

When a company files a Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) or Prospectus for an IPO, the analyst wants
an early, grounded first pass: three years of restated financials, and drafts of the analysis
sections, ready before listing day. Today Analekh has no source for unlisted companies, because
quarterly XBRL only exists after listing.

## Goals

1. Store the three-year financials (plus the stub period, if the document presents one) from an
   RHP or Prospectus, each figure traceable to its page and unit.
2. A "IPO Coverage" tab on the Reports page that shows those financials and generates an
   analyst-facing draft: MD&A, financial performance, sector performance, strengths and risks,
   and a valuation section. The rationale section and the rating are analyst input.
3. Keep versions: RHP, then Prospectus, then (after listing) quarterly XBRL. Later sources win,
   and a change is shown as a restatement.

## Non-goals

- No target price anywhere in the IPO report (owner, 29 October).
- No AI-written rating or recommendation (rule 8).
- No subscribe/avoid logic, no price forecast.
- No live IPO pricing or subscription data.
- No change to the existing listed-company report flow.
- No DRHP at all: the three stages of an offer are DRHP, RHP and Prospectus. Only the RHP and
  Prospectus are used. Drafting can be done from the RHP; the Prospectus, once filed, becomes
  the current document and supersedes it (owner, 29 October).

## Anti-goals

- A figure in the draft that is not in the document or from `calc` (rule 3). Fail closed.
- Any write into an analyst's model without an approved change set (rule 2).
- Any figure stored as a float (rule 4).
- Silent overwrite of an earlier RHP figure by a Prospectus figure.

## Constraints

- Source host: `www.sebi.gov.in`, limited by `ALLOWED_PATHS` to the list page
  (`/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do`, smid 10 to 12, newest page only, one request a day), entry
  pages (`/filings/public-issues/`, one per new filing), and PDFs (`/sebi_data/attachdocs/`).
  Approved 28 October. No other SEBI page, no crawling.
- Requests identify as Analekh (rule 1).
- Documents run 300 to 600 pages. The page classifier (`docs/pdf.py`) locates restated
  statements, the KPI section and the peer table.
- Money is Decimal, stored in full rupees (rule 4).
- All routes need a signed-in user; only admins manage templates (rule 9).
- GenAI draft text may contain only numbers from tool results or `calc` (rule 3, `grounding.py`).
- No em-dashes in drafts (rule 8).

## Decisions as they stand

1. **Financial years.** Store the three restated years presented in the document, and the stub
   period when present. Consolidated and standalone are both stored and shown, neither preferred
   (owner, 29 October).
2. **Units.** Each table records its stated unit. Amounts are converted to rupees. A check
   catches a table where units disagree with its neighbours (the V11 pattern).
3. **Versions.** RHP figures are version 1. Prospectus figures are version 2 and supersede
   version 1 for the displayed financials. Changed numbers are listed as restatements. Quarterly
   XBRL after listing supersedes both (V10).
4. **Company identity.** The IPO issue is its own record. NSE assigns the symbol once the
   company takes bids, so the analyst may enter it then. Otherwise, once the company is listed,
   the symbol is taken from NSE's bhavcopy file (owner, 29 October). Linking by symbol needs no
   extra confirmation step (rule 2 is about analyst models, not IPO records).
5. **Reports tab.** A new "IPO Coverage" tab. It takes a company in the IPO state and produces
   the sections below. Each section is generated by the existing report loop (flash, note style),
   one section at a time, with a grounding check per section.
6. **Sections.**
   - MD&A and financial performance: from the three-year table and the document's own MD&A text.
   - Sector performance: from the RHP's industry chapter.
   - Strengths and risks: from the RHP's risk factors and business section.
   - Valuation: multiples at the price band against the peers in "Basis for Offer Price", all
     computed with `calc`. No target price.
   - Rating: a marked placeholder for the analyst. The AI does not fill it.
   - Rationale: a marked placeholder for the analyst.
7. **Rating placeholder.** Shown as "[Analyst to set rating]" in the draft and in any export.
   Analekh stores no rating for IPO companies.

## Left to the builder

- Exact table-detection approach for restated statements (the classifier's output format).
- Storage shape for versions and restatements (new tables, migration via `new-migration`).
- How the stub period is labelled.
- Whether the rationale and rating placeholders are stored or only rendered.
- Test approach: synthetic fixtures only (rule: `tests/xbrl_factory.py` style); a real RHP
  becomes a golden case only after the owner marks it.

## Source flow (settled by the owner, 29 October)

- List pages: `/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=3&ssid=15&smid=N`, with smid 11
  (RHP) and 12 (Prospectus). Newest page only, once a day each. smid 10 (DRHP) is allowed by the
  access rules but is not fetched.
- Each list row links to an entry page, for example
  `/filings/public-issues/oct-2026/jio-platforms-ltd-prospectus_105427.html`.
- The entry page embeds the PDF from `/sebi_data/attachdocs/<month>/<id>.pdf`.
- The abridged prospectus is not used: not every company publishes one.
- SEBI's website terms: the owner accepts the current access (no API; the site was read openly
  in an earlier session). Not read line by line; the owner's call.

## Still to confirm while building

Settled by the live fetch of Jio Platforms Limited on 29 October (captures in `out/sebi-captures/`,
gitignored):

- Rows are `<tr><td>Mon D, YYYY</td><td><a href=ENTRY_URL title=...>TITLE</a></td></tr>`, newest first.
- An RHP row's title holds a nested abridged-prospectus link under `/sebi_data/commondocs/`
  (not an allowed path, not used). Take only the first anchor's `href` and its own text.
- Titles differ between stages: "Jio Platforms Limited – RHP" (en dash, 15 Oct) and
  "Jio Platforms Ltd.- Prospectus" (26 Oct). Both reduce to the stem "jio platforms".
- A Prospectus follows an RHP as a new entry with a new id (105274, then 105427).
- The entry page embeds the PDF as `<iframe src='../../../web/?file=https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/<id>.pdf'>`;
  the real PDF URL is the `file=` value.

## Owner's answers on the calls (29 October)

- Stub period included when present: yes.
- Consolidated and standalone: both, equally; neither preferred.
- Rating placeholder rendered, not stored: yes.
- DRHP: not used. Only the RHP and Prospectus are sources. Early coverage is drafted
  from the RHP and refreshed from the Prospectus (owner, 29 October).
